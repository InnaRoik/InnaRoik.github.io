About IK Laser BC

Hi, I’m Inna, the owner and certified laser technician at IK Laser BC. I have over 18 years of experience in the beauty industry, including almost 6 years operating spa services on different cruise lines, where I learned high standards of client care, hygiene, and professional service. Now I bring that same level of quality to my laser hair removal studio. I work one-on-one with every client, making sure you feel comfortable, informed, and well taken care of. My goal is simple: smooth skin, great results, and happy clients. Thank you for choosing IK Hair Laser BC — I look forward to seeing you!
